COVERLY
01 | Purpose and Current Product
The purpose of this report is to establish a reliable snapshot of what Coverly is building, the sourcing difficulties
it intends to address, whom it aims to serve and what progress has been made so far.
Coverly is a proposed next-generation B2B marketplace connecting business buyers with manufacturers,
wholesalers, suppliers and distributors. It is intended to bring provider discovery, quotation comparison,
purchasing and order management into a connected digital workflow.
THE CORE IDEA
Help businesses discover the right providers, compare offers according to
their requirements, and manage purchases and order progress in one place.

02 | Problem Area
Coverly is being developed around the sourcing and procurement process used by businesses that need to
purchase products, raw materials or other business supplies. The founder has identified three main problems
that Coverly aims to address:
01
Manual comparison
Comparing prices and product
details from different providers can
require effort and manual
organisation.
PROBLEM-STATEMENT STATUS
02
Scattered order
management
Managing purchases from multiple
providers can make it harder to
maintain one clear view of orders.
03
Delivery visibility
Tracking purchased products and
delivery progress across different
providers can be inconvenient.
These are the problems Coverly intends to solve. The founder has not yet completed research to establish how
common they are, how businesses currently handle them, or which competing platforms already offer similar
solutions.
03
Why the Problem Matters
When sourcing information, quotations and orders are spread across different providers and communication
channels, buyers may need to organise the information manually. This can make it more difficult to compare offers
consistently, maintain visibility over active orders and follow delivery progress.
PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS
Coverly’s product hypothesis is that connecting provider discovery, quotation comparison, purchasing and order
visibility in one workflow could make sourcing easier to organise. The size of this problem and the impact of the
proposed solution still need to be validated with real users.
POTENTIAL AREAS OF IMPACT
Time spent sourcing
Time may be spent finding providers and
collecting product or price information.
Order visibility

providers to check separate order statuses.

side-by-side comparison difficult.

multiple deliveries harder to follow.
