
What We Should Put on Coverly's Website (Section by Section) 💻✨
​To make the web platform super high-converting for MSMEs and buyers while staying lean and light on hardware, here is the blueprint of what sections you should build out:  
​1. Hero Section (The Hook)
​What to put: A punchy value proposition highlighting that Coverly is the modern wholesale marketplace for the next generation.  
​Key Elements:
​A bold headline like: "Stop the Sourcing Nightmare. Compare True Landed Costs Instantly."
​A quick call-to-action (CTA): "Explore Live RFQ" or "Get Verified Quotes".
​2. The Problem vs. Solution Overview (How It Works)
​What to put: A quick 3-step visual workflow breakdown (matching your pitch deck):  
​Step 1: Connect — Submit a structured requirement, and Coverly matches you with verified local suppliers.  
​Step 2: Compare — View a clean, side-by-side comparison table of true landed costs (price, MOQ, freight, and GST).  
​Step 3: Manage — Select the best fit, track follow-ups, and manage repeat orders in one unified dashboard.  
​3. The Interactive AI Feature Section (The WhatsApp-to-Table Parser)
​What to put: This is where you drop that killer visual reference above! Show how suppliers send messy WhatsApp quotes and photos, and Coverly's AI instantly turns them into clean comparison data. It immediately builds massive tech credibility.  
​4. Interactive Demo / Live Quote Preview
​What to put: Instead of a heavy video demo, give users an interactive mini-widget right on the page where they can simulate entering an RFQ for a sample product category and see how the comparison table populates.
​5. Trust & Verification Badge / Network Section
​What to put: Highlight your backing by Rungta Business Incubator and emphasize ground-truth verified local data to smash the market trust deficit.  
​6. Footer / "Talk to a Consultant" or Concierge Support
​What to put: Since early on you're running a manual concierge pilot to guarantee a 48-hour response time, include a simple form: "Book a Sourcing Concierge" or "Talk to Our Team" so early buyers and suppliers can reach out instantly via a clean lead form or direct chat link.
